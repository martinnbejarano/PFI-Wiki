\chapter*{Abstract}

This work addresses the spread of disinformation on social media and the lack of a free tool, adapted to the Argentine context, that allows users to assess the veracity of a post at the moment they read it. To that end, Factum was developed: a Google Chrome extension for Twitter/X that combines a natural language classifier fine-tuned on Spanish corpora, the public signals of the author's account, and the comparison of the claim against official sources, reference media outlets, and professional fact-checkers.

Each result comes with a justification and links to the sources that support it, so that users can verify it on their own. The solution was implemented as a functional prototype that integrates into the Twitter/X timeline and is complemented by a web dashboard, which gathers each user's analysis history and offers organizations an aggregated view of trends. On verified Argentine posts, the system's verdict reached a macro F1 score of 0.654, above the baseline, and the usability test obtained a mean SUS score of 74.2.
